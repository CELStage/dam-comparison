Best Choice

Recommended Platform
Bynder

Bynder passes every mandatory Must and earns the highest weighted Want score of the surviving options — though by a narrow margin (472 vs Brandfolder's 468, just four points). It leads on the most heavily-weighted criteria here — search (weight 10) and tech-stack integrations (weight 9) — while meeting our security and universal-access requirements. Brandfolder finishes just behind and stays very close, leading on analytics, pricing transparency and public-sharing UI/UX while tying Bynder on ease-of-use, and remains a strong alternative if contract flexibility (1-year vs Bynder's 3-year) or onboarding simplicity is weighted more heavily. Canto (394) and OpenAsset (317) follow. SharePoint and Adobe Bridge are eliminated: each fails multiple Musts (no flexible organization, no true single source of truth, limited universal access or adoption path).
