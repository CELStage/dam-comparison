4 The Decision

Best Choice

The platform that clears every Must and earns the highest weighted Want score.

🏆
Recommended Platform
Bynder

Bynder passes every mandatory Must and earns the highest weighted Want score of the surviving options — though by a narrow margin (472 vs Brandfolder's 468, just four points). It leads on the most heavily-weighted criteria here — search (weight 10) and tech-stack integrations (weight 9) — while meeting our security and universal-access requirements. Brandfolder finishes just behind and stays very close, leading on analytics, pricing transparency and public-sharing UI/UX while tying Bynder on ease-of-use, and remains a strong alternative if contract flexibility (1-year vs Bynder's 3-year) or onboarding simplicity is weighted more heavily. Canto (394) and OpenAsset (317) follow. SharePoint and Adobe Bridge are eliminated: each fails multiple Musts including flexible organization, single source of truth, and attainable DAM adoption.

5 The Close Call

Bynder vs. Brandfolder — The Tie-Breaker

The top two finished just four points apart out of 570 — still close enough that the weighted score alone shouldn't be the deciding factor. The real decision comes down to which trade-offs matter most to us.

Bynder

472 / 570
Why pick Bynder
  • Wins the two heaviest criteria: search (wt 10) and tech-stack integrations (wt 9)
  • Largest ecosystem — 164 marketplace apps plus an SDK/developer toolkit
  • Natural-language conversational search and AI Duplicate Manager
  • Built-in CloudFront CDN; triple analyst Leader (Gartner, Forrester, IDC); 24/7 support
Trade-offs
  • 3-year contract commitment — least flexible term
  • Quote-driven, modular pricing; cost can climb with add-ons and paid storage ($600/TB/yr beyond 1 TB)
  • Base plan caps users (2 admin / 10 regular); Light Users are view-only and additional packs are extra
  • At a similar price point, includes 1 TB of storage vs Brandfolder’s 5 TB
  • Can feel over-engineered for a lean marketing team

Brandfolder

468 / 570
Why pick Brandfolder
  • Leads on analytics, pricing transparency, and public-sharing UI/UX (and ties Bynder on ease-of-use)
  • More baked-in collaboration and sharing features at the same pricing point
  • Similarly priced package includes 5 TB of storage vs Bynder’s 1 TB — meaningful given storage add-on costs
  • 1-year terms, clearer Premium/Core pricing; G2 #1 rated and simplest to adopt
Trade-offs
  • No native natural-language search; quality leans on a good taxonomy
  • Some integrations carry one-time setup fees
  • No mobile app
  • Initial setup and tagging can be time-consuming
Lean Bynder if…

Search power and the broadest integration ecosystem are the priority, and a 3-year commitment is acceptable for the lowest annual cost.

Lean Brandfolder if…

Contract flexibility (1-year), pricing transparency, faster adoption, analytics, and external/public sharing UX matter more than raw search — especially if the extra storage (5 TB vs 1 TB) and collaboration features at the same tier reduce long-term add-on spend.
